Technical Methodology & Architecture (HackSHSID 2026)

1. Mathematical Optimization Objective Formulation

We formulate the data center co-scheduling problem as a dynamic multi-objective linear program over a discretized 24-hour horizon ($T = 24$):

Minimize J = w_cost * sum(P_grid(t) * C_tou(t)) + w_carbon * sum(P_grid(t) * I_grid(t))
  • $P_{\text{grid}}(t)$: Grid electricity power draw at hour $t$ (MW).
  • $C_{\text{TOU}}(t)$: Time-of-Use electricity price tariff ($/MWh).
  • $I_{\text{grid}}(t)$: Grid carbon intensity factor (kg CO₂ / MWh).
  • Weights ($w_{\text{cost}}, w_{\text{carbon}}$): Defined dynamically according to the selected priority policy.

2. Physical Energy Conservation & Hithium ESS Constraints

At every discrete time step $t$, physical power balance conservation is strictly maintained:

P_workload(t) = P_green_direct(t) + P_grid(t) + P_ess_discharge(t) * eta_dis - P_ess_charge(t)

Hithium ESS battery state of charge (SOC) dynamics:

SOC(t+1) = SOC(t) + [ P_ess_charge(t) * eta_ch - P_ess_discharge(t) / eta_dis ]

3. Workload Classification & Latency SLA Protection

Non-Deferrable Real-Time Serving

Strict SLA deadline requirements. Assigned immediate execution priority upon arrival with zero deferral window.

Flexible Batch High-Performance Computing

Delay-tolerant compute jobs. Queued & shifted to off-peak tariff hours or peak local solar output window.
