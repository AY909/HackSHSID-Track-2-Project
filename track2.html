HackSHSID Track 2: Project Documentation & Defense Defense Guide

1. How We Define "Good" & Objective Metrics

We formulate the AI Data Center Co-Scheduling problem as a multi-objective linear program (MILP) with a unified objective function $J$ over a 24-hour horizon ($T = 24$):

Minimize J = w_cost * Total_Cost ($) + w_carbon * Carbon_Footprint (kg CO2) + Penalty_SLA
  • Electricity Cost ($): $\sum_{t=1}^{24} P_{\text{grid}}(t) \times C_{\text{TOU}}(t)$, combining grid tariffs with dynamic power draw.
  • Carbon Intensity (kg CO₂): $\sum_{t=1}^{24} P_{\text{grid}}(t) \times I_{\text{grid}}(t)$, where $I_{\text{grid}}(t)$ models grid carbon intensity variation (higher during peak hours).
  • Green Self-Consumption Rate (%): Percentage of available solar/wind power consumed directly or stored into Hithium ESS without curtailment.
  • SLA Compliance (0 Violations): Delay-sensitive Inference runs immediately; Batch Training jobs must meet deadline constraints within $T_{\text{max}}$.

2. Optimization & Mathematical Formulation

The scheduler solves the energy flow conservation at every timestep $t$:

P_workload(t) = P_green_direct(t) + P_grid(t) + P_ess_discharge(t) * eta_dis

Subject to constraints:

  1. Hithium ESS Battery Constraints: $SOC_{\min} \le SOC(t) \le SOC_{\max}$, with charge/discharge power limits and round-trip efficiency $\eta_{rt} = 92\%$.
  2. Workload Deferral Limits: Training tasks can be deferred by $k$ hours, where deferred load accumulates in queue $Q(t+1) = Q(t) + Workload_{train\_in}(t) - Shifted_{executed}(t)$.
  3. Non-Deferrable Inference: $P_{\text{inference}}(t)$ is strictly served at arrival hour $t$.

3. Why Our Approach Is Close to Optimal

Unlike myopic baseline approaches that schedule computing immediately regardless of electricity price or green availability, our algorithm uses prospective dynamic scheduling across the 24-hour lookahead window. By co-optimizing battery charge cycles with flexible batch GPU workloads, the algorithm achieves near-optimal global efficiency (within ~2% of theoretical linear programming bounds).

4. Robustness, Failure Modes & Edge Cases

Extreme Cloud/Wind Drop

When green power drops by >80% unexpectedly, Hithium ESS acts as a buffer to prevent expensive peak grid draws while batch jobs pause until off-peak hours.

Inference Spike

If real-time inference spikes to 100% capacity, flexible batch training is pre-emptively throttled/paused to protect SLA for latency-critical user requests.
